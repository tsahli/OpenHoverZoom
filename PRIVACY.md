# OpenHoverZoom Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026

OpenHoverZoom previews and downloads images, videos, and audio from the pages you browse.

## Information handling

OpenHoverZoom has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or developer-operated backend. It does not send browsing history, page content, credentials, or personal information to the developer, and does not sell user data.

The extension inspects page elements and media links to find larger media and galleries. It may fetch a linked page to locate its media. This processing happens in your browser, and temporary results are held in memory.

Loading previews, fetching linked pages, and downloading media sends ordinary network requests to the websites hosting that content. Those websites receive your IP address and the requested URL, and may receive other information normally sent by your browser, including cookies where the browser allows them. These requests are necessary to display or save the requested content. Linked-page requests made by the extension's background worker omit credentials and referrers. Third-party websites handle requests under their own privacy policies.

## Settings and downloads

Preferences, keyboard shortcuts, and the list of sites you disable are saved locally using Chrome's extension storage. OpenHoverZoom does not sync these settings to a developer server. Uninstalling the extension removes its local settings. Downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them.

## Permissions

- **Website access:** Inspect thumbnails and media links on the pages you visit and request larger media or linked pages from their original hosts.
- **Storage:** Save your preferences locally in your browser.
- **Downloads:** Save media when you use the download shortcut.

OpenHoverZoom does not download or execute remote code or remote rules.

## Contact and changes

For privacy questions, open an issue at [OpenHoverZoom on GitHub](https://github.com/tsahli/OpenHoverZoom/issues). Do not include private browsing information in public issues.

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
